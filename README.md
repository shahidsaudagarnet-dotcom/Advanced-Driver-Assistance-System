ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) Project
Project Overview
This project focuses on the development and implementation of an Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS) to improve vehicle safety and driving efficiency. The system uses sensors, cameras, and intelligent algorithms to monitor the driving environment and assist the driver in making safer decisions.
Objectives
Enhance road safety.
Reduce the risk of accidents.
Assist drivers in maintaining vehicle control.
Provide real-time alerts and warnings.
Features
1. Lane Departure Warning (LDW)
Detects lane markings using camera input.
Alerts the driver when the vehicle unintentionally leaves its lane.
2. Forward Collision Warning (FCW)
Monitors the distance between vehicles.
Warns the driver about potential front-end collisions.
3. Traffic Sign Recognition (TSR)
Identifies traffic signs such as speed limits and stop signs.
Displays recognized signs to the driver.
4. Pedestrian Detection
Detects pedestrians on the road.
Generates alerts to prevent accidents.
5. Blind Spot Detection
Monitors areas not visible through mirrors.
Warns the driver when another vehicle is in the blind spot.
